Fictional training data generated by Finance & Commercial Training Simulator. Not real company or industry data.

Hotel: The Aurelia Grand Hotel - fictional 150-room luxury hotel, fiscal year 2025 (Jan-Dec).
Retail: Harbor Lane Outfitters - fictional apparel retailer.
Currency: US dollars. Revenue in daily files is to the cent; monthly P&L lines are rounded to whole dollars.
Ratios are stored as decimals (0.75 = 75%).

Story built into the data (for instructors):
  * From September, weekday corporate demand weakens and transient business shifts to OTAs at lower
    rates. Room revenue falls below budget and commissions rise.
  * April pay rise above plan; summer housekeeping overtime; July chiller repair; energy tariff
    increase from June; F&B food cost rises from September.

Files

                                     revenue, cancellations, no-shows, sold/revenue by segment
  hotel_daily_jan_with_issues.csv    January extract with a missing value, a closed day and a
                                     zero-sales day (edge cases for Module 3)

  hotel_payroll_2025.csv             headcount, average cost, overtime and payroll by department
